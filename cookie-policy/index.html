Cookie Policy

Effective 18 October 2026

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. This page lists what proclassicgroup.com stores and lets you change your choice. For how we use personal information in general, see our Privacy Policy.

Your choice

When you first visit, a banner asks whether you accept analytics cookies. Google Analytics cookies are only set after you click Accept. If you click Decline, they are not set, and Google may only receive basic signals that a page was viewed, without cookies.

Other ways to control cookies

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site still works if you do, but you may see the cookie banner again.
