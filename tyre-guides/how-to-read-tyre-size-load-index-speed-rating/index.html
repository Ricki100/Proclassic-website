How to Read Your Tyre Size, Load Index and Speed Rating

Every tyre tells you exactly what it is on its sidewall. Learn to read the size, load index, speed rating and date code, and you will always order the right tyre.

Quick answer

A tyre marking such as 215/65R16 98H means: 215 mm wide, sidewall 65% of the width, R for radial, for a 16-inch rim, load index 98 (750 kg per tyre) and speed rating H (up to 210 km/h). Truck tyres add a second load index for dual fitment, such as 315/80R22.5 156/150L. Tractor and OTR tyres often use inch sizes such as 18.4R38 or 23.5-25, where a dash means bias-ply. Always replace with the same size and the same or higher load index and speed rating.

Where to find the markings

The size is moulded in large characters on the sidewall of every tyre. You will also find it on a sticker inside the driver's door or fuel flap of most cars, and in the vehicle handbook. Read it from a tyre that is the right size for the vehicle, not from a spare that may be different.

Some SUV and bakkie tyres start with LT (light truck) or end with C (commercial), such as 195R15C. These have stronger casings for heavier loads. Some 4x4 tyres use inch sizes like 31x10.50R15: 31 inches tall, 10.5 inches wide, for a 15-inch rim.

Truck tyres: 315/80R22.5 156/150L

Truck sizes read the same way, with one addition: two load indexes.

  • 156 is the load index when the tyre is fitted on its own (single), usually on the steer axle.
  • 150 is the load index for each tyre when fitted in pairs (dual).
  • L is the speed rating (up to 120 km/h).

Older-style truck sizes such as 12R22.5 and 1200R20 give the width in inches. A 20-inch rim size usually means a tube-type tyre. Read more in our 315/80R22.5 guide and 12R22.5 guide.

Tractor and OTR tyres

  • 18.4R38: 18.4 inches wide, radial, 38-inch rim. With a dash (18.4-38) it is bias-ply.
  • 460/85R38: the metric equivalent, 460 mm wide with an 85% sidewall.
  • 23.5-25 L-5 20PR: a bias OTR loader tyre, 23.5 inches wide for a 25-inch rim, extra-deep rock tread, 20 ply rating.

Agricultural tyres often show a load index with a speed symbol such as A8 (40 km/h). See tractor tyre sizes explained and how to choose mining OTR tyres.

Ply rating and load range

Older and heavy-duty tyres show a ply rating such as 8PR, 16PR or 20PR. It no longer counts actual layers of material, but it still indicates casing strength: a higher ply rating carries more load at a higher pressure. Light truck tyres may show a load range letter instead, such as LR E (roughly 10 ply). Radial OTR tyres use stars.

Other markings worth knowing

  • Date code: a four-digit number such as 3825 means the 38th week of 2025.
  • TL / Tubeless or TT / Tube Type.
  • M+S (mud and snow) on all-terrain and some commercial tyres.
  • Max load and max pressure: the maximum the tyre can take, not the pressure you should run. Use the vehicle's recommended pressure.
  • Rotation arrow on directional tyres: fit so the arrow points in the direction of forward travel.
  • TWI or a small triangle marks where the tread wear indicators sit in the grooves.

The golden rules when replacing tyres

  1. Fit the size the vehicle maker specifies.
  2. Choose the same or a higher load index and speed rating.
  3. Fit matching tyres across an axle, and in pairs on duals.
  4. Do not mix radial and bias tyres on the same axle.

Ask on WhatsApp
What does 98H mean on a tyre?

98 is the load index, meaning the tyre can carry up to 750 kg. H is the speed rating, meaning up to 210 km/h.

Why do truck tyres have two load index numbers?

The first is for single fitment, such as the steer axle. The second is for each tyre in a dual pair, which carries slightly less because two tyres share the load.

Can I fit a tyre with a lower load index?

No. Always fit the same or a higher load index than the vehicle maker specifies, or the tyre may overheat and fail under load.

How do I tell how old a tyre is?

Find the four-digit date code on the sidewall. The first two digits are the week and the last two the year, so 3825 means week 38 of 2025.

What does a dash mean in a tyre size like 23.5-25?

The dash means bias-ply construction. An R in the same place, such as 23.5R25, means radial.
