How to read a bus or kombi tyre size
295/80R22.5

295 = width in mm · 80 = sidewall % · R = radial · 22.5 = rim in inches (tubeless).

195R15C

195 = width in mm · R = radial · 15 = rim · C = commercial: a stronger, higher-load tyre built for kombis and vans.

What tyres fit a Toyota Quantum kombi?

Toyota Quantum / HiAce kombis commonly run 195R15C or 195/70R15C commercial tyres. The “C” means a reinforced commercial tyre with a higher load rating — always fit C-rated tyres on a loaded kombi. Check the size on your current tyre’s sidewall and send it to us.

Can I use car tyres on a kombi?

No. Kombis carry heavy passenger loads, so they need commercial (C-rated) tyres with stronger casings and higher load indexes. Ordinary car tyres can overheat and fail under that load.

What is the difference between steer and drive bus tyres?

Steer tyres on the front axle have rib treads for stable, predictable steering and even wear. Drive tyres on the rear have deeper treads for traction and long mileage. Many operators fit premium tyres on the steer axle for safety.
